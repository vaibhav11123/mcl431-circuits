Privacy

Sequence Circuit does not create accounts.

Spec YAML sent to a hosted MCP is used to draw and check that one call. It is not stored by this software. No analytics.

On ChatGPT Free, Go, Plus, and Pro, OpenAI may use app content to train models when “Improve the model for everyone” is on.

Do not send payment-card data, health records, government identifiers, or credentials.

Lecture PDFs are never uploaded to the public pack.
