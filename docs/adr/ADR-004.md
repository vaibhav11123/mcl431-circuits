# ADR-004 Public ISO pack vs course pack

Status: accepted

## Context

The public product cannot ship lecture PNG crops.

## Alternatives

1. One pack for everything.
2. Two packs: `circuit.packs.iso_sequence` (vectors + two Wikimedia CC-BY rasters) and `circuit.packs.mcl431` (course lock).

## Decision

Public stamps are svgdraw vectors plus Wikimedia CC-BY 5/2 and 5/3. Lecture crops stay private. ISO `Glyph.path` must not resolve `lecture/crops`.

## Tradeoffs

Public glyphs are coarser than lecture stamps. Course goldens stay on the lecture pack.

## Revisit when

A third pack is required, or Wikimedia files are replaced.
