# ADR-008 Cascade is extra 5/2 valves

Status: accepted

## Context

Cascade appears in the course. FluidPyPLC is GPL. We must not copy it.

## Alternatives

1. Port FluidPyPLC.
2. Invent a 5/3 cascade glyph.
3. `meta.method: cascade` plus extra valves typed `dcv_5_2_double_solenoid`. More than three cylinders is refused.

## Decision

Cascade for ≤3 cylinders is extra 5/2 group valves. Do not copy FluidPyPLC. Closed `PatternId` enum.

## Tradeoffs

Four-cylinder cascade is a recovery sentence, not a guessed circuit.

## Revisit when

The ISO atlas adds a cascade-specific valve that the lecture actually uses.
