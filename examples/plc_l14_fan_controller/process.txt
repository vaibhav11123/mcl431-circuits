L14 Fan Controller — convert the question into logic (before rungs)
Source: MCL431_L14_PLC_Problems.pdf

What the paper wants
  • T 0–100 °C and RH 0–100% on 16-bit AI (Slot 2)
  • Three zones only (do not invent others):
      S1 = (T>30)∧(T<40)∧(RH>50)     → 200 rpm
      S2 = (T>40)∧(T<50)∧(RH>40)∧(RH<50) → 500 rpm
      S3 = (T>50)∧(RH<40)               → 800 rpm
  • Single PB alternates START/STOP (edge, not level)
  • Three lamps for selected speed
  • 10-bit AO: 100 rpm @ 1 V … 1000 rpm @ 10 V

I/O (Micrologix-style assignment used in circuit.yaml)
  I0.0 PB | AI Slot-2 Ch-0 T | AI Slot-2 Ch-1 RH
  AO Slot-1 Ch-0 | Q0.0 L1 | Q0.1 L2 | Q0.2 L3

Scaling (exam formulas)
  T  = (T_raw / N) × 100     (N = module raw max, e.g. 32767 or 65535)
  RH = (RH_raw / N) × 100
  V_out = RPM / 100
  Count = (V_out / 10) × 1023   → 205 / 512 / 818 for 200/500/800 rpm

Logic flow
  Read AI → scale T,RH → toggle FAN_RUN on PB edge →
  evaluate S1/S2/S3 → MOV AO (or 0 if none) → lamps follow Si

Toggle (conceptual)
  PB · ONE-SHOT → TOGGLE_EVENT
  if TOGGLE_EVENT: FAN_RUN := NOT FAN_RUN
  (Our ladder: shared ONS pulse + OTL/OTU on previous-scan RUN; see walkthrough.txt)

Zones (all series — AND)
  FAN_RUN · (T>30)·(T<40)·(RH>50) → SPEED_1 / 200 rpm
  FAN_RUN · (T>40)·(T<50)·(RH>40)·(RH<50) → SPEED_2 / 500 rpm
  FAN_RUN · (T>50)·(RH<40) → SPEED_3 / 800 rpm

AO / lamps
  V = 2 V / 5 V / 8 V for S1/S2/S3; else 0 V (conservative default)
  Lamp_i ON iff corresponding Si (and thus FAN_RUN) is true

Memorise: sensors → scale → FAN_RUN → compare → speed → AO → lamp
