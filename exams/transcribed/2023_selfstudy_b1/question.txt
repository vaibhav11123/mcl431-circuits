B1. In an Automated surface grinding machine, a top surface of the cube is to be grind by the
grinding wheel. The grinding wheel should approach job from a distance of 45 cm. On pressing a
START push button, the job is clamped on the machine with the help of hydraulic cylinder HC1
having 45 mm bore dia and 22.5 mm rod dia. After job is clamped to 2.5 kN force, the feed
cylinder HC2 having 40 mm bore dia and 20 mm rod dia will advance to make grinding wheel
touching the workpiece top surface. Grinding wheel is made to rotate at 400 RPM by Hydraulic
Motor HM1 before HC2 advance. Cylinder HC2 will retract after 30 sec of grinding and then HC1
unclamp the job. Draw Electro-pneumatic Circuit Diagram to achieve the above mentioned
functionality with one hydraulic pump. Use suitable sensors to detect the end position of the
actuators and presence of the job.
[15 Marks]
