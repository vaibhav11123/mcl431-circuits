B2. In a Sheet metal forming press, the shape to be formed is hemispherical cup of radius 19 cm in a mild steel sheet. The force required to overcome packing friction is 1.3 kN and to deform sheet is 6 kN. The forming hammer should approach job from a distance of 11 cm. Flow rates of Pump P1 and P2 are 400 cm3/s and 70 cm3/s respectively. Hydraulic cylinder has piston area Ap= 20 cm2 and rod area Ar = 6 cm2.
• Diameters of pipes: D1a = D1b = 3.7 cm, D2 to D8 = 2.5 cm.
• Length of pipes: L1 = 20 cm, L2 = 40 cm, L3 = 36 cm, L4 = 20 cm, L5 = 26 cm, L6 = 100 cm, L7 = 108 cm, L8 = 50 cm.
• Consider Laminar flow in pipes.
• K value of all Tee Joints = 3.8 and Elbow joints = 2.7. K value for Check Valve = 3.3, DCV = 4.2.
• Hydraulic Fluid: Kinematic viscosity = 105 cS, Weight density of oil = 9866 N/m3
• Frictional pressure losses in pipes, fittings, valves are negligibly small during the punching operation and can be ignored.
• Ignore small length of pipes for which dimensions are not given.
Find the Head Loss in Pipes, Valves and Fitting.
