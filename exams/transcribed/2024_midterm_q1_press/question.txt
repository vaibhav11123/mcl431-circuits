Q1. In a Sheet metal forming press, the shape to be formed is hemispherical cup of radius 35 cm in a mild steel sheet. The force required to overcome packing friction is 1.55 kN and to deform sheet is 7.65 kN. The forming hammer should approach job from a distance of 10 cm. Flow rates of Pump P1 and P2 are 250 cm3/s and 70 cm3/s respectively. Hydraulic cylinder has piston area Ap = 20 cm2 and rod area Ar = 10 cm2.
- Diameters of pipes: D1a = D1b = 2.2 cm, D2 to D8 = 1.4 cm.
- Length of pipes: L1 = 20 cm, L2 = 40 cm, L3 = 36 cm, L4 = 20 cm, L5 = 26 cm, L6 = 100 cm, L7 = 108 cm, L8 = 50 cm.
- Consider Laminar flow in pipes.
- K value of all Tee Joints = 3.1 and Elbow joints = 2.8. K value for Check Valve = 3.6, DCV = 4.5.
- Hydraulic Fluid: Kinematic viscosity = 115 cS, Weight density of oil = 9876 N/m3.
- Frictional pressure losses in pipes, fittings, valves are negligibly small during the punching operation and can be ignored.
- Ignore small length of pipes for which dimensions are not given.
Considering Head Loss in Pipes, Valves and Fitting, Find the pressure ratings of pumps P1 & P2 considering 82% overall efficiency.
