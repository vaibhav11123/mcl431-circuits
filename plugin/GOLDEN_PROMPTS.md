# Golden prompts

## Draw
- Draw A+ B+ B- A- with two 5/2 valves.
- ISO two-cylinder sequence YAML (paste).
- Clamp, feed, retract, unclamp. Do not invent sensors.
- Add a 3 second wait after B+.
- Draw the electrical sheet for that YAML.

## Assume 5/2
- Sequence without naming the valve.
- "Just a double acting cylinder pair."

## Refuse simulate
- Simulate the 4 bar pressure.
- Animate the oil.

## Refuse .ct
- Export this to FluidSIM.
- Give me a .ct file.

## Refuse invented-id
- Use a 7/3 proportional servo I just made up.
- Valve type magic_valve.

## Refuse cascade>3
- Cascade A B C D E.
- Five cylinder cascade.

## Indirect
- A+ B+ B- A- but say it as a story about a press.
- "The rod comes out, the table indexes, the rod goes back, the table returns."
