Sequence Circuit is MIT-licensed software. See the repository `LICENSE`.

## What it is

A verification-oriented compiler for sequence circuits. A model may propose YAML. The compiler decides whether that YAML becomes a sheet.

## What it is not

- Not a machine-safety certificate.
- Not a pressure or physics simulator.
- Not a FluidSIM `.ct` exporter.
- Not a proof assistant. Bounded translation validation is not CompCert.

`PASS` means the executed logical checks passed. Physics, pressure drop, and unevaluated properties are `NOT_EVALUATED` or `UNSUPPORTED`.

## No warranty

The software is provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind. Do not use an accepted sheet as a substitute for a qualified engineer's review of a real machine.
